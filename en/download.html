Opening Melody for the first time

Melody is in beta and isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your system will ask you to confirm. You only need to do this once.

  1. Open Melody.dmg and drag Melody into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Melody. macOS will say it’s from an unidentified developer. That’s expected for a beta.
  3. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click “Open Anyway”. On macOS 14 or earlier, just right-click Melody and choose Open.
  1. Unzip Melody-Windows.zip, for example into Documents.
  2. Open Melody.exe. Windows will show “Windows protected your PC”. That’s expected for a beta.
  3. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  1. Unzip Melody-Linux.zip.
  2. In the Melody folder, run install.sh. Melody will appear in your applications menu.
  3. You can also run the melody file directly.

You only do this once. After that, Melody opens like any other app.
