Features

Everything you need to make music

Compose, play, arrange, mix. Here’s what Melody can do today, and every update adds more.

ComposePlayArrangeMix and soundEverywhere
01

Compose

Start from a hummed melody, a chord progression or a blank page.

Melody Maker

Sing the moment an idea hits. Melody listens, picks out the notes and writes them as MIDI, ready for any instrument to play.

Harmonize

Starting from your melody, Melody suggests chords, then a bassline and drums to go with them.

Inspiration

Drums, a bassline, chords or a melody, generated in the key of your choice.

Audio to MIDI

Turn a vocal or instrument recording into notes you can edit.

Step-sequenced beats

Click the steps and the beat plays. Humanize the velocities for a more natural groove.

02

Play

Melody is built for the stage as much as the studio.

Made for live

Launch clips, move between scenes and improvise. Follow actions chain clips together for you.

Play it, it’s recorded

Your MIDI keyboard or your computer keys, recorded straight into your clips.

One, two, three…

A one-bar count-in before recording, so you have time to get ready.

Jam together

Work on the same song with other people, in real time. No account needed, just a code.

03

Arrange

From pattern to full song, with your own sounds and takes.

From pattern to song

Place your patterns in the Playlist and build the intro, verses and choruses, one section at a time.

Your takes in the song

Drop your recordings into the Playlist, trim them and add fades.

Always in time

Your sounds follow the song’s BPM without changing pitch.

One loop, endless ideas

Melody slices your loop at each hit. Play every slice back from your keyboard.

Bring it all to life

Automate any parameter, plugins included.

04

Mix and sound

A big sound from the start, and every tool you need to shape it.

A precise mix

A full console with volume, pan, effects and meters on every track, plus a limiter on the master.

A bigger sound

Built-in drums and a stereo synth with Width, Sub, Vibrato and Warmth.

A mix that breathes

Built-in sidechain, on any track.

All your plugins

Your VST3 and CLAP instruments and effects, plus free plugins you can install in one click.

To Live, Logic and beyond

Export as WAV, MP3, MIDI or individual stems.

05

Mac, Windows and Linux

The same studio on whatever computer you use. And a full demo in your browser, with nothing to install.
